Frequently asked questions

How much is my home worth?

It depends on location, size, condition, features, and what comparable homes are selling for right now. Megan prepares a comparative market analysis so your price is based on current local data.

How long does it take to sell a home in Arizona?

Timelines vary with price, condition, neighborhood, and season. Megan will share what she is seeing in your area when you talk.

Should I make repairs before selling?

Sometimes, and sometimes not. Some updates help a home show well, while others cost more than they return. Megan can help you decide which are worth doing for your home.

What happens during a home valuation?

Megan reviews your property details and photos, studies comparable sales, and may visit the home. You then receive a pricing recommendation and a conversation about strategy.

What costs should I expect when selling?

Common costs include agent compensation, title and escrow fees, any agreed repairs or credits, and prorated taxes. Megan will outline an estimate specific to your sale.

Do I need to buy another home before selling?

No. Some sellers sell first, some buy first, and some coordinate both. Choose "Sell and Buy Another Home" in the form and Megan will help plan the sequence.
